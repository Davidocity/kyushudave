## What and who is KyushuDave?

Hello! My name is David Cantrell. I live in Kyushu, and KyushuDave.com is where I share my travels, adventures, photography, and personal thoughts about Kyushu, Japan. If you plan to visit Kyushu or are simply interested in the region, you may find this site useful.

Thank you for visiting KyushuDave.com!

## About Me

I first came to Japan in 1988 and worked as a salaryman for a big, multinational Japanese company headquartered in Tokyo. Then I returned to Los Angeles, raised a family, did the career thing, and traveled back and forth between Los Angeles and Japan many times for both business and pleasure.

I returned to Japan in 2025 after more than three decades away—this time to live in Kyushu, explore its hidden corners, and figure out the next chapter of my life.

## Why Kyushu?

My wife’s family is from this region, and it's a great place to live.

Kyushu is one of Japan's most rewarding yet still under-the-radar destinations—not (yet) as packed with tourists as Tokyo, Osaka, or Kyoto. There is still a lot of the old Japan I remember from 1988 here in Kyushu, where visits from Western foreigners are not an everyday occurrence. And Kyushu offers world-class food, active volcanoes (including one of the world's largest calderas), spectacular hiking, historic towns, countless hot springs, beautiful coastlines, and a pace of life that feels very different from Tokyo and Osaka.

Historically, Kyushu served as Japan's gateway to the outside world. Today, it remains one of the country's most fascinating regions—and one of the least covered by English-language travel writers.

## Why Japan?

On a whim in 1988, I sent off a handful of letters to Japanese companies inquiring about employment. At the time, many people were projecting that Japan's economy would dominate the world. In college, I had become interested in Japan and dedicated many of my elective liberal arts courses to studying its history, literature, sociology, and business alongside my primary coursework. My letters generated a few interviews and a job offer!

Since 1988, Japan has been a constant thread throughout my life—professionally, personally, and now as a place to live. I've seen, and personally experienced, the Japanese economy rise, fall, and stabilize. I've watched its culture influence the world. And I've seen the country become one of the most popular travel destinations on earth.

## Why I Started KyushuDave

Over the last year and a half, I've spent a great deal of time traveling around Kyushu and searching online for new places to explore. While there are many tourism-board-style websites, I have yet to find one that combines travel insights with an authentic, personal perspective on the wonderful places, people, culture, and experiences that make Kyushu unique.

That's what I hope to create with KyushuDave.
